How to use

Start with the lessons. Each one shows a five-card hand, explains its pattern, and tells you what breaks a tie.

Use Back and Next, or select a numbered hand. The rankings run from 1 (strongest) to 10 (weakest).

Try the 10-question quiz when you are ready. Choose one answer, read the explanation, and continue. Your best three scores are saved on this device when storage is available.
