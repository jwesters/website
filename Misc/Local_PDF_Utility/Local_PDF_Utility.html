Local PDF Utility

Merge, split, and compress PDFs directly in your browser.

Local only
Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. PDF processing happens locally in your browser, and this app works offline.

Merge PDFs

Add two or more PDFs, put them in the order you want, then combine them into one file.

Drop PDF files hereor choose files from your device

Split a PDF

Extract pages, create custom parts, split every few pages, or save every page as its own PDF.

Drop one PDF hereor choose a file from your device
Use commas and ranges. Page order is kept as entered.
Separate parts with a slash, semicolon, or new line.
Each page will become its own PDF. All page PDFs will be downloaded together in a ZIP file.

Compress a PDF

Clean the PDF structure and, on stronger settings, reduce the size and quality of embedded JPEG images.

Drop one PDF hereor choose a file from your device
Compression results vary. Text and vector content stay intact; Medium and Strong can soften images. Password-protected PDFs are not supported.
No account. No upload. No server-side storage.
