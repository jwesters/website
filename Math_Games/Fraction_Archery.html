Fraction Archery

Use the numbers 1–10 to build three fractions as close as possible to the decimal targets. Every number can be used only once. Lowest score wins.

How to Play

Build one fraction for each target decimal. Use each number from 1 to 10 at most once across the whole round. You do not have to use all ten numbers, but all three fractions must be complete before you submit. Your score is the total error × 1000, so a lower score is better.
