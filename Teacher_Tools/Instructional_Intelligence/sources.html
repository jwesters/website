Sources & Project Notes

This library brings together classroom strategies, routines, structures, and organizers from a range of teaching sources. The explanations have been written specifically for this project, and the printable resources were created for teachers to use with students.

Where the project started

Beyond Monet: The Artful Science of Instructional Integration by Barrie Bennett and Carol Rolheiser was one of the main starting points for the library. It was especially useful for distinguishing between instructional strategies, tactics, cooperative structures, skills, and organizers.

What belongs in the library

The focus is on approaches teachers can actually use with students to shape thinking, discussion, comprehension, collaboration, practice, and meaning-making. Broad philosophies, administrative systems, and lesson-planning frameworks were left out unless they clearly describe what students experience during instruction.

Using the PDFs

The PDFs in the library are original classroom resources made for this project. Teachers can print them and share them with students or colleagues.
