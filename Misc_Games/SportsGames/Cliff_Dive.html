How to Play

Time the moving water, avoid the rocks, and make the cleanest entry you can.

LaunchGrab the diver, drag toward the water, and release. A longer drag gives more power.
FlipA / Left = backflip. D / Right = front flip. More than half a flip earns bonus points; backflips are worth more.
SpinUse W/S. Spinning earns bonus points, but makes the flip harder to control.
Water entryA clean headfirst entry earns full points. A clean feet-first entry counts for half points. Use the Male/Female button to swap diver style instantly.
StarsThree floating stars give 100 / 300 / 600 total. A rare shooting star gives 300 points.
ScorePoints come from distance, airtime, entry straightness, flips, spins, and stars. Each retry creates a new fair rock layout.
