THE RULE BOOK

HOW TO PLAY

01
BREAK + CLAIM YOUR GROUP

Break the rack, then become solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15) when you legally pocket a ball on a later shot. The table stays open after the break.

02
POCKET YOUR BALLS

Hit a ball from your group first. After that contact, a ball must reach a cushion or be pocketed. Pocket your own ball legally to shoot again.

03
WIN ON THE 8-BALL

Once all seven of your balls are gone, select a pocket and sink the 8 there to win. Sinking the 8 too early, into the wrong pocket, or while scratching loses the game.

04
FOULS + BALL IN HAND

Scratches, wrong first contact, or failing to drive a ball to a cushion after contact give the other player ball in hand anywhere on the cloth. An illegal break also gives ball in hand.

05
CONTROLS + SPIN

Desktop: Move the mouse over the table to aim freely—no click needed and no power meter yet. Your first left-click or first Space press locks your aim and starts the moving power meter; a second click or second Space press shoots at the current power. The on-screen button works the same way: START POWER, then TAKE SHOT. If you change your mind before shooting, choose CANCEL SHOT or press Esc / C to stop the meter and go back to free aiming, without losing your turn. The top of the meter is the hardest hit. Once locked, the cue stays put as you move toward the Shoot button. Drag the mouse or right-click to change your aim without firing. Use ← / → for fine aim; Esc opens Pause when no shot is being prepared. Touch: Tap once to aim and reveal power; tap again or use TAKE SHOT to shoot. Tap CANCEL SHOT to back out and aim again. Drag a finger on the table to adjust aim. Drag + Release mode: Pull backward and release to shoot, or use the same two-tap method. On the spin ball, tap or drag to add follow, draw, or side spin; keyboard users can focus it and use arrows or Home to reset. Use Help (?) or Settings (⚙) for more options, including aiming assistance.

06
ARCADE RULES NOTE

Uses standard-style American 8-ball fouls, open table, re-spotted 8 on the break, and called 8-ball pocket. Ordinary object-ball pockets are accepted without explicit shot calls to keep play quick.
