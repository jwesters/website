Rules

  • Cover every cell on the board.
  • Rectangles are axis-aligned and can’t overlap.
  • Each rectangle contains exactly one clue.
  • Rectangle area equals its clue number.

Progress

Each difficulty has 20 fixed levels. Beating a level unlocks only the next level in that same difficulty. Progress is saved locally on this device.

Controls

Drag on the grid to place a rectangle.

Click inside a rectangle to delete it.

Undo removes only your most recent rectangle.
