How to play

Match pairs and keep the board alive as long as you can.

  • Match equal numbers or two numbers that total 10.
  • Pairs must connect horizontally, vertically, on a true diagonal, or from the last cell of one row to the first cell of the next. Cleared cells may sit between a pair on the same straight path.
  • Whenever a row is fully cleared, it collapses upward and two fresh rows are added immediately at the bottom. Automatic rows do not use an Add.
  • Add Line always adds exactly one new row of 9 numbers. You get 10 Adds for the entire run.
  • Hint shows one legal pair. You get 5 hints for the entire run. When both Adds and Hints reach 0, a Give Up button appears.
  • Daily Run uses the same UTC seed for everyone. Random Run creates a fresh run.
