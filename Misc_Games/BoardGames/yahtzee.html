Rules

Each turn, roll five dice up to three times. Click dice to hold them between rolls.

After rolling, score one open category. The game ends after all 13 categories are filled.

Upper section bonus: score 63 or more across Ones to Sixes to earn +35.

Yahtzee is five of a kind for 50 points. After scoring 50 in Yahtzee, each extra Yahtzee earns a +100 bonus.

Extra Yahtzee rule: score the matching upper category first if it is open. If it is filled, lower categories can use Joker scoring.
