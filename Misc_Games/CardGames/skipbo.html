Start a game

Choose the classic turn-based game or the custom simultaneous Speed mode.

Traditional

Take turns. Play as many legal cards as you can, then discard one card to end your turn.

Speed

You and the computer play at the same time and race to empty your stock pile first.

Rules & Help

Goal

Be the first player to empty your 30-card stock pile.

Shared building piles

  • There are four shared building piles.
  • Each pile begins with a 1 and builds upward in order to 12.
  • A wild card can stand in for any number.
  • When a pile reaches 12, it clears and can begin again at 1.

Traditional mode

  • You draw up to five cards at the start of your turn.
  • You may play the top card of your stock pile, the top card of any discard pile, or any card from your hand.
  • If you play all five cards from your hand, you immediately draw five more and keep playing.
  • To end your turn, place one card from your hand onto one of your four discard piles.

Speed mode

  • After a 3–2–1–GO countdown, both players can play simultaneously.
  • Both players use the same four building piles.
  • If both players want the same next number, whichever card reaches the pile first gets the play.
  • Your hand refills to five immediately whenever it becomes empty.
  • You may put hand cards onto your discard piles at any time.
  • Difficulty changes the computer's reaction speed and decision-making.

Controls

On phones and tablets, tap a card and then tap a highlighted destination. You can also drag cards on supported touchscreens and desktop. In Traditional mode, dragging/tapping a hand card onto one of your discard piles ends your turn.
