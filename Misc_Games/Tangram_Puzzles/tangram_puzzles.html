How to Play

Copy pieces from the palette and fit them into the outline.

Add a pieceDrag a colorful piece from the palette onto the board, or tap it to add a copy.
MoveDrag any piece already on the board.
RotateUse the 45° buttons, the mouse wheel, or Q / E.
FlipParallelograms can be mirrored with Flip or F.
SnapA correctly oriented piece snaps into its spot when it gets close.
HintBriefly highlights one unsolved piece location.
SolutionShows the internal arrangement without moving your pieces.
