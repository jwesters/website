How to Play

Movement

  • W / ↑Move forward one maze cell.
  • S / ↓Move backward one maze cell.
  • A / ←Turn left 90°.
  • D / →Turn right 90°.
  • MToggle the explored map on or off.
  • EscClose the map or Help window.

On phones and tablets, use the on-screen Forward, Back, Turn Left, Turn Right, and Map controls below the 3D view.

Map Rules

  • Only maze cells you have actually visited are revealed.
  • Unvisited maze areas stay hidden.
  • The green start marker and red finish marker are always shown.
  • Your current position is shown with an arrow that points in the direction you are facing.
  • As you explore new cells, their floor area and surrounding walls are added to the map.
