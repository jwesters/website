Jump over holes and dodge obstacles. Birds start appearing at level 2 and begin swooping at level 7. Frogs join at level 10 with two small hops and one huge leap. After level 12, planes fly through the middle too.

Controls: Space / W / Up Arrow to jump. A/D or Left/Right to move.
