QUICK FIELD GUIDE

How to play

↔
Steer into the gaps

Use A / D or ← / →. On a phone, use the big on-screen arrows. The ball has real momentum!

↑
Jump to escape

Press Space / ↑, or tap JUMP, right after touching a platform. Leap over obstacles and aim for its opening.

↓
Dive for speed

Hold S or ↓ (or the DIVE button) to fall faster. Watch where you're headed!

◉
Platforms bounce you back

There are no lives to lose. Platforms are solid from above and below, so jumps cannot pass through them. Steer for the openings.

↗
Bumpers & springs

Orange bumpers knock the ball away. Bright green springs launch you high. Some float in midair between platforms—watch your descent!

◷
Collect power-ups

Rarer turquoise clocks add 3 seconds (up to 18 bonus seconds per run). Purple bolts give 5 seconds of extra speed. Collect both kinds as you fall.

✦
Get fancy

Clear holes without hitting platforms for combo points, and build longer clean-drop streaks.

✿
Spinning paddles

Purple rotating arms can smack the ball off course. Time your drop around them.

⇄
Sliding walls

Golden panels slide in from the edges, briefly squeezing your route.

⌁
Break-through floors & crumbling platforms

Sometimes a platform seals the entire way down. Land on the amber BREAK HERE patch to crack it, then drop through when it falls away. Other cracked ledges also collapse on contact.

◕
Swinging pendulums

Hanging wrecking balls sway across your descent and send you flying.

➜
Wind tunnels

Blue updrafts swirl around platform openings, pushing you sideways and slightly upward. Steer against the gust to line up your fall.

⚡
Pulsing barriers

Red energy rails turn on and off. An active rail bounces you away.

FARTHER = WILDER: More bumpers and springs appear the deeper you go, and the new obstacles become more common. Every route stays passable. P / Esc pauses. Classic repeats its course; Shuffle generates a new one.
