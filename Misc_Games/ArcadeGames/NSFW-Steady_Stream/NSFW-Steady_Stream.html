How to play

Control the wobble.

The body is a wobbly pendulum. A and S shift the hips independently from the planted legs, changing both balance and where the stream lands. Sometimes one leg lifts on its own, forcing you to shift weight and settle it back down.

Strong correctionQ left foot pressure · W right foot pressure
Hip aimingA shift hips left · S shift hips right
MissesMissed shots splatter, stain the room lightly, and build the mess meter.
Game overYou lose by falling or by filling the mess meter.
