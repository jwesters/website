Prototype

Swap neighbouring gems, score enough points before the timer runs out, and climb as many stages as you can.

8×8 board · Local highscores

Prototype rules

🍬Stages 1–10: 4 colours. Stages 11–20: 5 colours. Stages 21–25: 6 colours. After that, a new colour appears every 5 stages until there are 9.
⏱️Level 1 starts at 25 points in 60 seconds. The goal rises by 5 points each level. More time is added at the bigger point milestones.
✨Match 3 = 3, match 4 = 5, match 5+ = 10. Cascades after the first clear get bigger multipliers.
💡One free hint is added each level. Unused free hints carry over up to 3. Paid hints cost 5 total points.
