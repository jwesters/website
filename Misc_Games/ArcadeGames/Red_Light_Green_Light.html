HOW TO PLAY

MoveWASD or arrow keys. On mobile, drag the joystick.
Bump PlayersRunning into another player moves them. They can bump you too.
Green LightRun for the finish. The countdown warns you when red is coming.
Red LightThe visible scan shows where the robot is looking. You may still move while the scan is elsewhere.
CoverRocks, trees, barriers, and other players can block the robot's actual line of sight.
LevelsYou have 1 minute. Once only the final elimination group is left behind, they are eliminated immediately. Survivors advance to a reshuffled, harder course.
Finish EarlyYour finishing place is shown when you cross. While you wait for the others, the rest of the round runs at 1.5× speed.
Between LevelsThe next level waits for you. Press Okay when you are ready to continue.
DifficultyEasy uses the current rules. Hard makes CPU players more aggressive and activates two scanning beams during red light.
