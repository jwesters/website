    How to Play

    • Each piece is randomly a horizontal row or a vertical column of three gems.
    • Cycle the gem order while the piece keeps its current orientation.
    • When the row lands, each gem falls independently into its own column.
    • Match 3 or more identical gems horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
    • Falling gems can create chain reactions for bigger scores.
    • The speed increases after every 15 gems cleared.
