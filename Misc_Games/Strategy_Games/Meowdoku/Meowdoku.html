How to play

Use logic, mark the impossible tiles, and give every region exactly one cat.

  • Place exactly one cat in every row.
  • Place exactly one cat in every column.
  • Place exactly one cat in every coloured region.
  • Cats may not touch, including diagonally.
  • Click, tap, or drag to mark tiles with ×.
  • Double-click or double-tap a tile to place a cat.
  • An incorrect cat costs one life and becomes a locked × for that attempt.
  • Optional assists can shade ruled-out tiles or mark them automatically with ×.
