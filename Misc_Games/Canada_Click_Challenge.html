Rules
You get 1 click per round and 30 seconds to answer. A correct click inside the city area earns 1000 points. You lose 1 point for every 2 km away from the nearest point on that city area, with a minimum of 0. If time runs out, that round scores 0.

After every guess, the game reveals the correct city area, your click, and the round score. At the end, you’ll get a challenge link with the same seed so others can play the exact same cities.
