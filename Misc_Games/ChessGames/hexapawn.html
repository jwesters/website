    Hexapawn Rules

    Hexapawn is a tiny strategy game played with pawns only. In this version, you can play on boards from 3×3 up to 8×8.

    How pawns move

    • Player 1 starts at the bottom and moves upward.
    • CPU / Player 2 starts at the top and moves downward.
    • A pawn may move one square straight forward if that square is empty.
    • A pawn may capture one enemy pawn diagonally forward.

    How to win

    • Move one of your pawns all the way to the opposite side.
    • Capture every enemy pawn.
    • Make a move that leaves your opponent with no legal moves.

    Forced Capture option

    When Forced Capture is on, a player must make a capture if any capture is available. If more than one capture is available, the player can choose which capture to make.

    CPU difficulty

    • Medium looks for wins and useful captures, but it can still miss deeper traps.
    • Hard searches ahead with a stronger strategy, especially on smaller boards.
