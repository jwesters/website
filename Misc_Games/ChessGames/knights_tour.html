Knight's Tour

Visit as many squares as possible. Each square can only be used once.

Board Setup

Changing the board size starts a new layout. Scores are saved separately for each exact layout.

Game Controls

How To Play

  1. Click any square to place your knight.
  2. After that, only green squares are legal moves.
  3. Try to visit every square once.
  4. When no legal moves are left, your score saves automatically.
