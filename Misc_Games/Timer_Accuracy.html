Hit Zero

Listen to the countdown in your head, then click when you think the timer hits 0.

How to Play

A number from 5 to 10 counts down once per second. The last two numbers are silent, so 2 and 1 will not appear. Click the big panel when you think the countdown reaches 0. You will play 5 rounds. Each round scores how many milliseconds off you were, whether early or late. After round 5, all 5 scores are added together. Lower is better.
