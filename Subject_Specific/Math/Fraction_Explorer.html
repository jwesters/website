How to use Fraction Explorer

Explore: Choose multiplication or division, enter proper, improper, mixed or whole numbers, and switch between four visual models. Watch the worked steps update.

Lessons: Read an explanation, advance the example one step at a time, and use Solve Along With Me to complete each stage yourself.

Practice: Select operations, number types, difficulty and answer style. Reveal hints or worked solutions when you choose. A revealed answer is marked as assisted.

Progress: View past scores or print a report. Information stays in this browser's local storage; private/incognito browsing or clearing browser data may erase it.

Typing answers: Enter 3/4, 1 1/2 or 2. Equivalent fractions are accepted. Division by zero is not allowed.
