# Game Library

**Release date: October 18, 2026**

ArcadeWeaver Studio provides **360 selectable game configurations**, spanning arcade games, puzzles, simulations, strategy games, and action games. Some selections use related gameplay engines with different themes, rules, or difficulty; these are not 360 unrelated implementations.

## Selected gameplay families

- Orchard Harvest Catch — catch falling produce while avoiding spoiled items.
- Deep Sea Diver — collect treasure while monitoring oxygen.
- Traffic Control Central — operate traffic signals and manage collisions.
- Shepherd Sprint — gather wandering animals into a pen.
- Rail Switch Yard — route trains through track switches.
- Drone Courier Run — transport packages while conserving battery power.
- Magnet Crane Operator — collect and sort scrap metal.
- Kitchen Orders Rush — assemble food orders in the correct sequence.
- Lunar Rover Expedition — drive over rough terrain and collect samples.
- Library Sorter — carry books to matching shelves.
- Pipe Connect Puzzle — rotate plumbing sections to complete a water network.
- Aquarium Care — maintain water quality and feed aquatic animals.
- Helicopter Rescue — carry survivors to a medical station.
- Orchard Picker — select ripe fruit from trees.
- Fossil Sorting Lab — categorize museum artifacts.
- Pizza Oven Rush — prepare, bake, and serve pizzas.
- Snowplow Patrol — clear winter roads while avoiding traffic.
- Subway Sprint — change lanes, dodge barriers, and collect tickets.
- Campsite Setup — gather essential supplies before sunset.
- Ant Colony Forage — carry food to the colony while avoiding hazards.
- Telescope Tracker — track celestial objects and photograph them.
- Bee Pollination Flight — collect nectar and return it to the hive.
- Museum Security Sweep — collect artifacts while avoiding guard patrols.
- Mail Pigeon Delivery — fly through aerial delivery markers.
- Harbor Fishing — cast, hook and reel in fish with timing controls.

Other catalog entries include first-person shooting, platforming, racing, puzzles, sports, pet care, animal rescue, farming, and management games. The searchable catalog inside the app lists all selectable configurations.
