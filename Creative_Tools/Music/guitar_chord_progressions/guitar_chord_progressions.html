How it works

Choose a progression, key, and difficulty. Easy favours open and low-position shapes. Medium adds movable and higher-neck voicings.

Change the display between diagrams, six-string fret codes, chord tones, and tab-style notation. Use the arrows on any chord card to cycle through alternate voicings.

Custom progressions accept Roman numerals I–VII, accidentals such as bVII or #iv, and qualities such as 7, maj7, m7, dim, °, m7b5, sus2, sus4, add9, 6, and 9.

Playback uses a lightweight synthesized guitar-like pluck. PDF export is generated locally and includes the current progression plus recommended chord diagrams.
